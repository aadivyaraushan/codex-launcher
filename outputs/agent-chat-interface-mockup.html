What dies: the approval sheet, the question sheet, the capability sheet, “one tap left” as a separate screen state.  What they become: agent messages in the thread, with the preview inline; you reply by tapping a suggested reply or just typing.  What stays: Android's own permission screens (notification access), the app drawer, the tiny state dots on thread rows so a glance still shows what needs you.
