# UIUC minimum monthly food spend (math)

**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**For:** Rough floor on how little you can spend to eat for a month at UIUC  
**Sources:** [housing.illinois.edu/cost](https://housing.illinois.edu/cost) (2025–26 and 2026–27 meal plan rates and guest meal prices); Champaign grocery ranges from student cost-of-living guides (~$200–$350/mo typical)

## Inputs → Outputs → Algorithm

**Inputs:** Where you live (dorm with required meal plan vs apartment/cook), how many meals/day, how often you eat out.  
**Outputs:** Monthly $ floor for food only (not rent).  
**Algorithm:** Price each path with official meal-plan rates or a grocery basket; divide academic-year plans by ~8 dining months.

## Academic year length used

Meal plans cover fall + spring dining (~late Aug → mid-Dec, mid-Jan → mid-May) ≈ **8 months / ~32 weeks**.  
Monthly figures below = academic-year price ÷ 8 (what you pay while school is in session).

## Path A — Cook everything (apartment / kitchen access)

This is the real **minimum** if you’re not forced onto a meal plan.

### Bare-bones Aldi-style basket (estimate, not a receipt)

Assumes ~3 meals/day, almost no eating out, shop Aldi/Walmart, rice–beans–eggs–pasta base:

| Item (month) | Rough $ |
| --- | ---: |
| Oats / breakfast starch | 4 |
| Rice + pasta | 12 |
| Beans / lentils (dry or canned) | 10 |
| Eggs (2–3 dozen) | 10 |
| Peanut butter + bread/tortillas | 12 |
| Fruit (bananas/apples) | 12 |
| Frozen vegetables | 15 |
| Cheap protein (chicken thighs / ground turkey) | 30 |
| Milk or alternative | 8 |
| Oil, salt, sauce, spices (amortized) | 10 |
| **Total** | **~\$123** |

Round up for waste/spice restocks → **~$150/month absolute floor**.

### Sustainable frugal (still cook, not miserable)

Add cheese, yogurt, better produce, occasional sauce/snack → **~$200–250/month**.

Champaign student guides typically quote **$200–350 groceries**; CostOfCity low grocery estimate for Champaign is ~**$202**.

| Level | Monthly food |
| --- | ---: |
| Extreme minimum (possible, hard) | **~$150** |
| Realistic minimum | **~$200–250** |
| Comfortable cook-at-home | **~$300–350** |

## Path B — Undergrad residence hall (meal plan required)

You **cannot** do Path A alone. Cheapest required undergrad plan is **12 Classic + 15 Dining Dollars**.

| Year | 12 Classic + 15 DD (academic year) | ÷ 8 months |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2025–26 | $5,772 | **~$722/mo** |
| 2026–27 | $6,062 | **~$758/mo** |

That’s food only as billed on the plan (room is separate in the standalone table; in undergrad halls room+board is one package and the 12-meal option is the cheapest board tier).

**Note:** 12 meals/week ≈ 52 meals/month. If you eat 3×/day (~90 meals), you still need ~40 meals from Dining Dollars, snacks, or groceries on top — so true minimum is **plan cost + maybe $50–100** unless you stretch 12 swipes carefully (big lunch + big dinner, skip breakfast).

---

## Path C — Grad / apartment optional meal plan (cheapest plan)

Available to Daniels/Sherman/University Apartments (not typical undergrad halls):

| Plan (2026–27 resident) | Academic year | ÷ 8 months | Meals/week |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 6 Classic only | $2,872 | **~$359** | 6 |
| 6 Classic + 25 DD | $3,602 | **~$450** | 6 + $25 flex |

6 meals/week ≈ 26 dining-hall meals/month. You still need groceries for ~60+ meals → add Path A’s **$100–150** → total roughly **$460–510/month**, which is **worse** than cooking-only.

---

## Path D — Pay cash at dining halls (do not do this for minimum)

Guest/credit prices including tax (2025–26 schedule on housing site):

- Breakfast $12.25  
- Lunch $15.26  
- Dinner $19.48  

**3 meals/day × 30 days = $47/day × 30 ≈ $1,410/month.**  
Worst “official” path.

---

## Bottom line

| Situation | True monthly food floor |
| --- | ---: |
| Can cook (off-campus / apartment) | **~$150 extreme / ~$200–250 realistic** |
| Undergrad dorm (required plan) | **~$720–760** (2025–26 / 2026–27 12-meal plan) |
| Cash dining halls 3×/day | **~$1,400** |

**Absolute minimum at UIUC if you have a kitchen: about $150–200/month.**  
**If you’re in an undergrad hall: about $750/month is the floor**, because the meal plan is mandatory.

## How to reuse

1. Confirm housing type (required meal plan or not).  
2. Pick Path A or B.  
3. Recheck [housing.illinois.edu/cost](https://housing.illinois.edu/cost) each spring when new rates post.

## Path E — Eat out every day, cheapest food that’s still fairly healthy (added 2026-08-05)

**Rule:** Restaurant / fast-casual / campus spots only (no home cooking). Prioritize protein + veg + carbs; skip soda, guac upsells, delivery fees, desserts.

### Verified / listed campus-area prices (pickup, not delivery)

| Spot | Cheap-healthy pick | Listed price | Notes |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Jerusalem (Wright) | Falafel / hummus sandwich | ~$4.95–$5.95 | Menu aggregators; platters ~$8–11 |
| Burrito King (Green) | Lunch burrito / fajitas | ~$7.50–$8.99 | Lunch menu on Beyond Menu |
| China King–style lunch specials | Chicken + veg + rice | ~$8.99 | Weekday lunch hours |
| Maize | 3 antojitos (happy hour deal) | $10 | Or ~$4/taco |
| Chipotle Green St | Bowl: chicken, beans, fajita veg, salsa, **no guac** | ~$10.80–$12.35 | Varies by list; in-store often nearer ~$11 |
| Y-Thai (YMCA) | Stir fry / curry / pad Thai | ~$12 | Official menu |
| Cravings | Chinese entrée | often under ~$10 | Student reports; not a fixed official list |

Add ~8–10% tax where not included → round meals up by ~$1 in the daily totals below.

### What “fairly healthy” means here

- Yes: bowls/plates with beans or lean meat, veg, rice; falafel + salad; stir-fry with veg; Maize tacos with beans/veg/meat.  
- Avoid as daily staples: deep-fried General Tso’s every day, nachos, pizza, soda meals.  
- Breakfast: egg sandwich / oatmeal / bagel+egg (~$4–6), water free.

### Daily templates → monthly

**Template 1 — Strict cheapest healthy (3 meals)**  
Breakfast $5 + lunch $8 + dinner $11 = **$24/day**  
× 30 = **~$720/month**

Example day: Dunkin/Einstein-style egg item → Jerusalem falafel sandwich or Burrito King lunch → Chipotle bowl (no guac) or Cravings veg-heavy plate.

**Template 2 — Realistic floor most people can sustain**  
Breakfast $5 + lunch $9 + dinner $12 = **$26/day**  
× 30 = **~$780/month**

**Template 3 — Two big meals (skip café breakfast)**  
Big lunch $10 + dinner $12 = **$22/day**  
× 30 = **~$660/month**  
(Works if you tolerate coffee/water mornings.)

**Template 4 — Delivery / DoorDash every meal**  
Add ~$4–8 fees+tip per order → easily **+$200–400/month**. Avoid for minimum.

### Month range (eat out, healthy-ish, consistent)

| Style | $/day | $/month |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Aggressive (2 meals, lunch specials + bowls) | ~$22 | **~$660** |
| Strict 3-meal cheap healthy | ~$24 | **~$720** |
| Comfortable cheap healthy | ~$26–28 | **~$780–840** |
| Chipotle-every-meal lifestyle | ~$33+ | **~$1,000+** |

### Compared to other paths

| Path | Monthly |
| --- | ---: |
| Cook (Path A realistic) | ~$200–250 |
| Eat out cheap healthy (this path) | **~$660–780** |
| Dorm 12-meal plan | ~$720–760 |
| Cash dining hall 3×/day | ~$1,410 |

**Takeaway:** Eating out every day on the cheapest *healthy* Campustown rotation lands around **$650–800/month** — roughly the same as the cheapest dorm meal plan, and about **3× cooking**.

### Caveats

- Lunch specials are often weekday-only; weekends push you to full-price (~+$2–4/meal).  
- Menu aggregator prices drift; treat ±10% as noise.  
- “Healthy” is relative: falafel is fried; Chinese lunch often includes fried egg roll — still better than pizza/soda every day.
